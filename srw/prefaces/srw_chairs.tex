Welcome to the Student Research Workshop (SRW) of the 15th International Joint Conference on Natural Language Processing and the 5th Conference of the Asia-Pacific Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (IJCNLP-AACL 2026), held in Hengqin, Zhuhai, China, on November 6, 2026.

The SRW provides a venue for student researchers in computational linguistics and natural language processing to present their work and receive feedback from the research community. Following the established SRW model, we invited two types of submissions: research papers, describing completed work or work in progress whose first author is a current student, and thesis proposals. Submissions were accepted both directly and through commitment of ACL Rolling Review (ARR) reviews.

This year the SRW received 78 submissions and accepted 41 papers, an acceptance rate of 52.6\%. Each direct submission received at least three reviews. We are grateful to our reviewers, who provided the constructive feedback that students value most in this workshop. Of the 41 accepted papers, 38 appear in these proceedings; the remaining three were presented at the workshop as non-archival contributions.

The SRW sessions took place on November 6, 2026, in two sessions (11:00--12:30 and 15:30--17:00) in Meeting Room 7 of the Tianmu Melody Convention Exhibition Center.

We thank the AACL-IJCNLP 2026 General Chair, the Program Chairs, the Publication Chairs, and the Local Organizing Chairs for their support throughout the organization of the workshop. We also thank our faculty advisor, Professor Yixin Cao, for his guidance. Most of all, we thank the student authors for sharing their research with the community.
